Bloc de notas brings notes, ideas, shopping lists, tasks, a Trello-style board and a calendar together in one app, designed for jotting things down instantly from your home screen.

<b>Instant capture</b>
• Quick-capture widget, icon shortcuts and a quick settings tile
• Inbox to sort things out later
• Share text from other apps

<b>Organize your way</b>
• Notes and checklists with colors and pinned notes
• Tasks with dates, priority, labels and reminders
• Board with columns and subtasks
• Calendar with agenda
• Saved views, templates and shopping mode

<b>Privacy first</b>
• Everything stays on your device
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics
• Backup and export to ZIP and Markdown
• Free software (GPL-3.0)
