Bloc de notas brings notes, ideas, shopping lists, tasks, a Trello-style board and a calendar together in one app, designed for jotting things down instantly from your home screen.

<b>Instant capture</b>
• Quick-capture widget with voice notes, icon shortcuts and a quick settings tile
• Inbox to sort things out later
• Share text from other apps

<b>Organize your way</b>
• Notes and checklists with colors, pinned notes, images (gallery or camera) and voice notes
• Tasks with dates, repeat, priority, labels, reminders and task dependencies
• One board per project, with columns and subtasks
• Calendar with month, agenda and timeline views, holidays and saints' days
• Saved views, your own templates and a shopping mode with aisles and quantities
• Dashboard summarizing your notes, tasks and ideas

<b>Privacy first</b>
• Everything stays on your device; optional sync with your own WebDAV/Nextcloud server
• Fingerprint lock and trash
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics
• Backup and export to ZIP and Markdown
• Import from Google Keep (Takeout) and Todoist (CSV)
• Free software (GPL-3.0)
